## Prompt specifications

All generated images requested premium realistic cinematic artwork with no text, UI, borders or watermarks, using the supplied mockup only as composition and style reference.

1. **Hero:** Wide midnight navy rocky landscape. Giant metallic glass A on right half with thin luminous icy blue orbital ring, distant planet and clouds, subtle gold trails. Left half dark negative space for HTML headline.
2. **Legal:** Square majestic metallic scales of justice in an emerald chamber, glowing interconnected nodes and laser network lines. Scales in upper two thirds and dark lower area for HTML overlay.
3. **Production:** Wide modern robotic factory, articulated arm at precision workbench centre-right, receding stations, cyan and blue lighting, steel machinery and glass partitions. Dark lower area for HTML overlay.
4. **SunShot:** Square barren alien terrain, huge planet with golden sunlit rim on right, star-filled navy sky, copper sunrise. Dark terrain below for product copy.
5. **Moon:** Wide barren lunar valley and mountains, light on horizon, distant planets, dark navy and steel gray atmosphere. No structures or humans.
6. **Founder:** Illustrative editorial portrait inspired by the male portrait in the supplied mockup: South Asian young adult, black turtleneck and tailored dark blazer, profile facing right, warm vertical office lights, dark negative space and realistic restrained lighting. Not a verified portrait of the founder.

7–14. **Supporting portraits:** Each generated individually as a square corporate portrait with one South Asian adult, natural skin, centered chest-up framing, face in upper half, subtle warm office window light and blurred charcoal/beige background. The subjects vary in gender, age, hair, glasses and clothing to match the reference layout. Board subjects use dark business attire; team subjects use navy or charcoal casual professional attire. All are explicitly anonymous illustrative portraits with no text, names, logos or watermarks; appointments remain unannounced.
